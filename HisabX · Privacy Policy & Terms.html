1. Summary

HisabX does not ask you to create an account and does not send the amounts you type or your saved history to any server of ours. The App works on your device.

2. What we collect

  • Amounts and calculations you type are processed on your device only.
  • Saved history (amount, GST, total, rate and time) is stored in local storage on your device, only if you tap “Allow”. You can turn this off or delete entries at any time in History.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or payment details through the App.
  • The Android app asks only for the Internet permission, which is used to load the font and to open links you tap. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The App loads a font from Google Fonts. When it does, your device connects to Google, which may receive technical details such as your IP address. This is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. Without internet the App still works and uses a standard font.
  • Links. If you tap a link to our websites (arduinometrix.in, shadownex.co) or open a phone or email link, that other site or app handles your information under its own policy.
  • Clipboard. The “Copy” button uses your device clipboard only when you tap it.

4. How we use data and who sees it

Because history stays on your device, we do not see it, share it or sell it. If you email or call us, we use your details only to reply and support you.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved history stays until you delete entries, turn the option off, or clear the App’s data (Android Settings → Apps → HisabX → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the App. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you.

6. Security

We keep the App simple and local to reduce risk. Anyone with access to your unlocked device could see saved history, so please protect your device with a screen lock.

7. Children

The App is a general calculator and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child’s data has reached us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, get your grievance addressed, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. For anything we hold (for example, emails you sent us), write to us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version, and the new version will be shown in the App.
